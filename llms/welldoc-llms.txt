# Welldoc

> Welldoc is a Columbia, Maryland digital health company whose FDA-cleared, AI-driven
> cardiometabolic care platform — best known through its BlueStar digital therapeutic for type 1
> and type 2 diabetes — delivers personalized coaching and clinical decision support across 30+
> chronic conditions. It sells to health plans, health systems, employers, life science companies,
> DMEs and medtech partners. Welldoc markets SDK and API access as a partnering option but
> publishes no developer portal, API reference, or machine-readable contract.
